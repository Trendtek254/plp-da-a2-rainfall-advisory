# #plp-da-a2-rainfall-advisory
*****.A farmer-support NGO faces challenges when sending SMS advice to farmers on planting windows accross five counties based on the collected rainfall readings from local weather stations.In this project i cleaned the rainfall readings dataset,performed calculations and analysed the data using python and Numpy .This helped me in determining the rainfall patterns.I compliled the report into a csv summary which shows the wettest month,number of dry months and two consecutive months with the highest rainfall per county which will in turn help the NGO in sending planting windows advisory to farmers.****

****Planting Advisory***

-1.Farmers in Nairobi,Nakuru,Kisumu and Mombasa are advised to to prepare their farms ahead of the April-May rainfall period ,which recorded the highest combined rainfall in these counties.

-2.Farmers in Machakos should prepare for November-December rainfall period instead which recorded the highest rainfall in the county.

-3.Kisumu recorded the highest annual rainfall of 1,371mm,making proper drainage important to minimize waterlogging.

-4.Machakos recorded the lowest annual rainfall of 811mm and six dry months,highlighting the importance of water conservation and drought preparedness.
